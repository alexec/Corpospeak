# Corpospeak Support

_Last updated: 11 September 2026_

Corpospeak listens to you talk, rewrites it as fluent corporate English, and reads the result
back in your own voice. It runs on a Mac, an iPhone or an iPad, and everything it does happens
on the device.

## Report a problem

Email me at <alex@alexecollins.com>. You do not need an account anywhere to do it, and I
would rather have a short email than nothing.

Say what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead. These help:

- the device, and the version of macOS, iOS or iPadOS on it
- the version of Corpospeak, from the App Store listing
- which voice was speaking: your Personal Voice, Kokoro, or one of Apple's built-in ones

## What to expect

I write these apps on my own, in my own time, so this is not a support desk. I read
everything that comes in. A reply usually takes a few days, and a fix takes longer than a
reply.

## Things that are working as intended

- **"Apple Intelligence is unavailable."** The rewrite runs on Apple's on-device model, so it
  needs an Apple silicon Mac, an iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with an M1 or A17 Pro
  chip or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.
- **It will not listen.** Corpospeak only ever recognizes speech on the device. If your
  language has not been downloaded, it tells you and stops rather than sending your voice to
  Apple's servers.
- **Your Personal Voice is not in the menu.** Record one in Accessibility → Personal Voice,
  then allow Corpospeak to use it. Until you do, it speaks with Kokoro.
