# Corpospeak Support

_Last updated: 10 September 2026_

Corpospeak listens to you talk, rewrites it as fluent corporate English, and reads the result
back in your own voice. It runs on a Mac, an iPhone or an iPad, and everything it does happens
on the device.

## Report a problem

Open an issue: <https://github.com/alexec/Corpospeak/issues>

Say what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead. Three things make it much
easier to find:

- the device, and the version of macOS, iOS or iPadOS on it
- the version of Corpospeak, from the App Store listing
- which voice was speaking: your Personal Voice, Kokoro, or one of Apple's built-in ones

## What to expect

I write these apps on my own, in my own time, so this is not a support desk. I read every
issue. A reply usually takes a few days, and a fix takes longer than a reply.

## Three things that are working as intended

- **"Apple Intelligence is unavailable."** The rewrite runs on Apple's on-device model, so it
  needs an Apple silicon Mac, an iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an M1 or A17 Pro iPad or later,
  with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.
- **It will not listen.** Corpospeak only ever recognises speech on the device. If your
  language has not been downloaded, it tells you and stops rather than sending your voice to
  Apple's servers.
- **Your Personal Voice is not in the menu.** Record one in Settings → Accessibility →
  Personal Voice, then allow Corpospeak to use it. Until you do, it speaks with Kokoro.
