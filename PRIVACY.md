# CorpSpeak Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 September 2026_

CorpSpeak does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the app does with your microphone

CorpSpeak listens through the microphone so it can turn what you say into CorpSpeak and read
it back to you. Speech is transcribed on your Mac with Apple's Speech framework, rewritten on
your Mac with Apple Intelligence, and spoken on your Mac with your Personal Voice. Audio and
transcripts are processed in memory and are not saved to disk.

On Macs where on-device speech recognition is not available for your language, Apple's Speech
framework may send audio to Apple for recognition under Apple's privacy policy. CorpSpeak has no
access to that data beyond the transcript it receives.

## Personal Voice

CorpSpeak uses the Personal Voice you create in System Settings → Accessibility → Personal
Voice, only after you allow it. The voice stays on your Mac and under macOS's control. You can
withdraw CorpSpeak's access at any time in the same System Settings pane.

## Network

CorpSpeak makes no network connections. It has no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no
third-party services.
